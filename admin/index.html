Power per level (1 to 7)

Each level has one FIXED power (not a random range) — every card created at, or merged up into, that level gets exactly this power. "Max power" is the separate upgrade cap: boost/sacrifice bonus power can raise a card above its level power, but never past this cap. Max power must always be ≥ the level's power, or the card would already be at its cap and impossible to upgrade.
